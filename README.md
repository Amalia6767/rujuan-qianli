# 入卷：千里江山 · A Walk into the Scroll

A short browser game (about 4–6 minutes) for AMCC 5160 Assignment 3, HKUST.
You walk into a living version of Wang Ximeng's *A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains*:
take in the view from a high bank, help a boatman, cross the long bridge and climb to the summit,
where a young painter is finishing his scroll.

**Play:** https://amalia6767.github.io/rujuan-qianli/

**Controls:** W A S D to walk (Shift to hurry) · drag the mouse to look · V to frame a view · E to interact · M to open the scroll.
Best on a desktop browser with sound on.
